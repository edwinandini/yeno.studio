Privacy

Clear, minimal data use.

yeno.studio uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand broad patterns such as page visits, article reads, and contact-link clicks. This helps us improve the site and understand which content is useful.

We do not sell personal information. If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide only to respond and discuss the work you asked about.

Analytics providers may process technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location, and interaction data according to their own policies. You can limit analytics through browser privacy controls or content-blocking tools.

For questions about this notice, email hello@yeno.studio.
